About the Sanctuary

Perched on the edge of the Sabi River, Mwakwanda Plains Lodge offers an unparalleled fusion of architectural elegance and raw wilderness. Our sanctuary is designed to breathe with the landscape, featuring open-air corridors and reclaimed materials that celebrate the spirit of the savanna. Here, luxury is defined by space, silence, and the profound connection to the earth.
